Read → review → apply

  1. Prepare the mapping. One broker, account and currency per file. Keep original documents, stable record locators and real execution IDs. One execution per fill row; do not convert order totals into invented fills.
  2. Create an opening. For a new account, choose Standard CSV on the account creation page (in the local TAD app / 在本机 TAD 应用中). Upload activity and optionally opening/current positions, review identity and dates, then confirm complete starting holdings and cash. A current snapshot alone does not explain past trades.
  3. Review before applying. “Archived” means stored, not included in calculations. “Pending update” means an unapplied draft. Inspect source rows, costs, cash, unknown fees and partial coverage; calculate and explicitly apply when correct.
  4. Continue through account updates. Use Upload files (in the local TAD app / 在本机 TAD 应用中), confirm the existing account, select one activity and/or current positions file, then continue the draft. Repeated identical source occurrences are not counted again. Conflicting corrections and uncertain overlap remain pending for review.

Supported here: USD equities and standard 100-multiplier US equity options. No adjusted deliverables, FX conversion, futures, crypto, order aggregates or strategy/P&L summaries as transaction inputs. Native ↔ Standard switching requires reviewed association, not a second account.

读取 → 核对 → 应用

  1. 准备映射。 每份文件只含一个券商、账户和币种。保留原件、稳定行定位及真实成交编号。每行对应一次真实成交，不要把订单汇总拆成编造的成交。
  2. 建立起点。 新账户在建账页 (in the local TAD app / 在本机 TAD 应用中)选择 Standard CSV。上传流水和可选期初／当前持仓，核对身份日期，再确认完整期初持仓及现金。只有当前快照不能解释历史交易。
  3. 先核对，再应用。“已归档”仅表示保存；“待更新”表示尚未采用的草稿。核对原始行、成本、现金、未知费用和部分覆盖，计算后再明确应用。
  4. 后续使用账户更新。 进入上传文件 (in the local TAD app / 在本机 TAD 应用中)，确认已有账户，选择一份流水及／或当前持仓，继续草稿。同一来源记录不会重复计入；冲突更正及无法证明的重叠保留待核。

本版范围：USD 股票及乘数100的标准美股期权。不支持调整交割、换汇、期货、加密资产、订单汇总或以策略／盈亏汇总代替流水。原生与标准来源切换需先核对关联，不能再建一个账户。

Unknown is not zero. Cash is not income.

Current valuation rows require cost_amount, cost_basis, price, market_value, unrealized_pnl and quote_as_of. If unavailable, keep unknowns blank and retain the document for review; this version cannot apply an unqualified valuation row. Activity amounts and fees may remain unknown with visible scope limits.

Leave unavailable values blank. A literal 0 must be supported by the source. Net cash already includes fees; commission is part of fee_total. TAD checks price, quantity, multiplier and signed amounts in the shared calculation core. Supplied fees stay partial/unknown in scope and are not certified complete.

A CASH book balance is a dated observation. It does not create a deposit, profit, buying-power allocation or a second pool of money. Partial snapshots do not close missing positions. An expiry/assignment/exercise quantity may close inventory, but missing proceeds and resulting P/L stay unknown.

Converted hashes, locators and snapshot_complete=true are declared mappings, not independent validation of an original or complete trading history. TAD may display a supported subset with visible limits. Review unresolved items before relying on totals.

未知不等于零，现金不等于收益。

当前估值持仓行必需 cost_amount、cost_basis、price、market_value、unrealized_pnl 和 quote_as_of。缺失则保持留空并保留文档待核，本版不能采用未满足条件的估值行。流水金额及费用可保持未知，并显示相应范围限制。

未知值留空，明确的0须有原件依据。net_cash 现金已含费用，佣金是总费用的一部分。TAD 在共享核心检查价格、数量、乘数和有符号金额；费用范围仍为部分／未知，不因填值就证明完整。

CASH 账面余额是有日期的观察，不创造新增入金、收益、购买力分配或第二份资金。部分快照未列出的仓位不会自动平仓。到期／指派／行权数量可以结束库存，但缺失终止金额及相应盈亏仍保持未知。

原件哈希、行定位及 snapshot_complete=true 都是映射声明，不代表独立核验原件或完整历史。TAD 会展示可支持的子集及其限制；依赖总额前请核对待核项。

Use AI carefully, if you choose

AI conversion is optional and runs outside TAD. Prefer a local tool. Sending originals to a cloud AI is a separate, voluntary data transfer; TAD does not upload them for you. Never include credentials or full account numbers in a prompt when a consistent local reference will do.

Conversion prompt and verification checklist
Convert only explicit source records to tad.standard-csv.v1 using the exact two headers. Keep original files unchanged. Map one real execution per fill row; never invent execution IDs, times, fees, proceeds, costs, completeness or transactions. Keep blanks unknown and distinguish trade/post/settlement/snapshot/quote dates. Preserve stable source identity across repeated exports. Produce a mapping log with original row locators, unknown fields, skipped/unsupported rows and counts by activity/account. Stop on conflicts. Do not calculate strategy P/L or capital efficiency in the CSV.
  • Compare every activity count and quantity to the original, including identical-valued separate fills.
  • Check signed net cash and fees against totals; reject TOTAL/buying-power rows as CASH.
  • Verify explicit option terms and multiplier; reject adjusted contracts.
  • Verify the earlier opening versus current snapshot dates, actual account identity and partial coverage.
  • Inspect TAD’s read-check rows and unresolved items before explicit apply; retain the mapping log and original.

如选择使用 AI，请保留核对环节。

AI 转换是可选的，在 TAD 外进行，优先使用本地工具。将原件发送到云端 AI 是你另行选择的数据传输；TAD 不会代你上传。可使用稳定脱敏引用时，不要在提示中提供凭据或完整账户编号。

转换提示与核对清单
仅将原件明确记录按两份准确表头转换为 tad.standard-csv.v1。原件保持不变，每行成交对应一次真实执行。不要编造成交编号、时间、费用、终止金额、成本、完整性或交易。未知留空，区分成交／入账／结算／快照／报价日期。重复导出保留稳定来源身份。另给映射日志：原始行定位、未知字段、跳过或不支持记录、分账户业务数量。遇到冲突停止。不要在CSV计算策略盈亏或资金效率。
  • 逐项比对原件业务次数及数量，包括金额相同的不同成交。
  • 核对有符号净现金及费用，TOTAL／购买力不能作为 CASH。
  • 确认期权明确条款及乘数；不转换调整合约。
  • 确认更早期初与当前快照日期、真实账户身份及部分覆盖。
  • 明确应用前查看 TAD 读取明细及待核项，保留原件和映射日志。

A small example you can verify
