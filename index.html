01 / STRATEGY ANALYSIS01 / 策略分析

What did each strategy contribute?每种策略，分别带来了什么？

Compare stock and option contributions, including supported LEAPS and CC. Explore PMCC support through long-Call and short-Call records. Confirmed relationships and complete strategy returns depend on the available evidence.比较股票与期权的收益贡献，包括有记录依据的 LEAPS 和 CC。联系长期 Call 与短 Call，查看 PMCC 支撑情况；关系确认和完整策略回报取决于现有依据。

  • Compare realized income across traded strategies and the securities involved.比较交易过的策略及其涉及个股的已实现收益贡献。
  • Inspect purchase costs, P/L and qualified whole-trade returns; follow open, partly closed and closed trades.查看买入成本、盈亏与符合条件的整笔交易回报，追踪持有中、部分平仓和已闭环交易。
  • Explore capital use, holding time and supporting assets in the explanations.在说明中进一步了解资金占用、持有时间与期权支撑资产。

02 / PER-STOCK ANALYSIS02 / 个股分析

One stock. Every supported contribution.一只个股，看清各项已支持的贡献。

Bring stock transactions and related options together. Find open holdings and closed trades, then inspect the gains, losses and costs behind each result.联系股票交易和相关期权，找到仍在持仓与已经平仓的交易，进一步查看各项结果背后的盈利、亏损和成本。

  • Separate stock gains or losses from LEAPS and other classified option-strategy contributions.分开看股票自身的盈亏，以及 LEAPS 等已分类期权策略的收益贡献。
  • Review realized contributions for the selected period; inspect current holdings and unrealized P/L with their saved dates.按所选期间回看已实现贡献，另行查看带保存日期的当前持仓与浮盈亏。
  • Narrow by broker, account and period, then follow a contribution back to its trades and evidence.按券商、账户和期间缩小范围，再从贡献追溯到交易与来源依据。

A call premium is only part of the story.收到 Call 权利金，只是故事的一部分。

A stock or long Call can lose more than the short Call earns. Read the supporting asset alongside the short leg; some strategy results remain partial. TAD marks those gaps beside the analysis.股票或长期 Call 的亏损可能超过短 Call 收益。结合支撑资产回看短腿；部分策略结果仍不完整，缺项会在分析旁标明。

Some strategies currently show only part of the result. TAD marks those gaps beside the analysis.部分策略目前只能展示部分结果，缺项会在分析旁标明。
