TWO WAYS TO UNDERSTAND YOUR TRADING两个视角，看清自己的交易

From strategy to stock. From results to understanding.横看策略，纵看个股，理解收益如何形成。

A strategy view asks what each approach contributed. A stock view brings the approaches used on the same underlying together.策略视角回答每种做法带来了什么；个股视角把同一标的上采用过的不同做法联系起来。

01 / STRATEGY ANALYSIS01 / 策略分析

What did each strategy contribute?每种策略，分别带来了什么？

Compare contributions from stock trades and supported option strategies, including LEAPS Calls, covered calls, PMCC and spreads where the records establish the classification.比较股票交易与已支持期权策略的收益贡献，包括有记录依据的 LEAPS Call、备兑看涨（CC）、PMCC 和价差策略。

  • Compare realized income across traded strategies and the securities involved.比较交易过的策略及其涉及个股的已实现收益贡献。
  • Inspect purchase costs, P/L and qualified whole-trade returns; follow open, partly closed and closed trades.查看买入成本、盈亏与符合条件的整笔交易回报，追踪持有中、部分平仓和已闭环交易。
  • Explore capital use, holding time and supporting assets in the explanations.在说明中进一步了解资金占用、持有时间与期权支撑资产。

02 / PER-STOCK ANALYSIS02 / 个股分析

One stock. Every supported contribution.一只个股，看清各项已支持的贡献。

Review stock trading, underlying option strategies and dividends together, including securities you have already exited. See what each recorded approach added to that security’s result.综合回看股票交易、以该股为底层的期权策略与分红，也包含已清仓个股。了解每种已记录的交易方式对该标的结果的贡献。

  • Separate stock gains or losses from LEAPS and other classified option-strategy contributions.分开看股票自身的盈亏，以及 LEAPS 等已分类期权策略的收益贡献。
  • Review realized contributions for the selected period; inspect current holdings and unrealized P/L with their saved dates.按所选期间回看已实现贡献，另行查看带保存日期的当前持仓与浮盈亏。
  • Narrow by broker, account and period, then follow a contribution back to its trades and evidence.按券商、账户和期间缩小范围，再从贡献追溯到交易与来源依据。

Stock trades lost money. LEAPS made money. What does that mean?股票交易亏损，LEAPS 盈利，怎么理解？

They are different contributions on the same underlying. Review the stock and option strategies separately, then inspect the supported security-level result. A positive capital-efficiency figure for a matched subset does not mean the stock’s overall result was positive.它们是同一标的上的不同收益来源。先分开看股票与各项期权策略，再查看有依据的个股综合结果；某个已匹配子集的资金效率为正，并不代表该股整体盈利。

Contributions follow supported records and classifications; unresolved items remain visible. CC/PMCC contribution may cover short-Call income only, with supporting stock or long-Call P/L shown separately. Period realized income, current unrealized P/L and whole-trade return retain their own scopes.收益贡献依据已支持记录和策略分类，未确定部分仍然可见。CC／PMCC 贡献可能仅覆盖短 Call 收入，支撑股票或长期 Call 的盈亏另列。期间已实现收益、当前浮盈亏与整笔交易回报分别保留各自口径。
